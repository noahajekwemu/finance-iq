# Finance IQ Model Series

**Finance IQ with Noah Ajekwemu**

Finance IQ Model Series is the flagship financial-modelling portfolio within the Finance IQ ecosystem.

The series demonstrates how finance professionals translate business problems into practical financial models, forecasts, scenario analysis, dashboards and decision-support tools.

> **Finance IQ teaches how finance professionals think. Model Lab demonstrates how finance professionals solve problems.**

## Purpose

Each Finance IQ model is designed to address a real business problem and demonstrate practical capability in:

- Corporate finance
- FP&A and forecasting
- Financial modelling
- Scenario analysis
- Business intelligence
- Executive reporting
- CFO-level decision support

Models are designed for professional use in Excel and, where practical, Google Sheets.

## Model Standard

Each flagship model follows a consistent architecture:

1. Business problem
2. Assumptions
3. Inputs
4. Calculation engine
5. Scenario analysis
6. Executive dashboard
7. Executive interpretation
8. Decision recommendations
9. Documentation and audit notes

The emphasis is not simply on producing numbers. The objective is to create a model that helps management understand what is happening, why it is happening, what could happen next, and what action may be required.

## Finance IQ Model Lab Workflow

Future releases will follow the same development process:

1. Define the business problem.
2. Design the workbook architecture.
3. Build inputs and assumptions.
4. Construct the calculation engine.
5. Develop scenario analysis.
6. Build the CFO dashboard.
7. Interpret the results and develop decision recommendations.
8. Perform quality assurance and documentation.
9. Release the model as a Finance IQ intellectual asset.
10. Develop supporting educational and authority content.
